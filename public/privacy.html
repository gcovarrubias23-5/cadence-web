Cadence

Privacy

Last updated September 24, 2026.

Cadence is a meal and water app for one person. This page is what we do with information today.

What stays on your phone

Your age, height, weight, how you move, your goal, foods you skip, the week of plates, which plates you ticked, water glasses, and weight logs are saved on your device. We do not have a Cadence account server for that yet. If you clear the site or delete the app, that history can go with it.

What we do not collect now

  • We do not create a login on the welcome screen.
  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use your plates to advertise other products.
  • We do not ask for contacts, photos, or your location to run the kitchen.

When that will change

If you subscribe, we will need an email or an App Store account so the paid kitchen can follow you. Payment is handled by Apple or by the payment company on the web, not stored as a card number inside Cadence. We will update this page before that ships.

Health

Cadence estimates calories and macros from the facts you type. It is not medical care, not a diagnosis, and not a promise about weight. Talk to a clinician if you have a condition that changes how you eat.

Kids

Cadence is built for adults. We do not aim it at children under 13.

Ask us

Questions about this page: use the email you will list in App Store Connect as the privacy contact.
